# Task 2.3 – Why choose Logistic Regression instead of a DNN/BERT model?

Logistic Regression with TF-IDF features is lightweight, fast to train, and suitable for a proof-of-concept with a modest dataset. It requires less computing power and is easier to interpret and maintain than training a deep neural network from scratch. A DNN or BERT model generally needs substantially more labelled data, compute resources, and tuning to train effectively. The conventional ML model therefore provides a practical baseline that can be evaluated before considering more complex models.
